# Re-engagement email — inactive sign-ups

Note: PageZaper doesn't have transactional/marketing email wired up yet (no `nodemailer` or similar in the codebase) — this is copy to send manually (your own inbox, or a bulk tool like Brevo/Mailchimp if you load the list in), not something the app sends automatically yet.

---

**Subject line options:**
1. Your PageZaper page is almost ready — one step left
2. Quick help setting up your page?

---

**Body:**

Hi {{name}},

I noticed you signed up on PageZaper but haven't set up your page yet — just wanted to check in.

It only takes a few minutes to get something live: add your bookings, a course, an event, or a digital product to sell, and your page is ready to share. Whatever you're trying to sell or offer, PageZaper is built to get you there without needing a developer.

👉 [Go to your dashboard]({{dashboard_link}}) and pick up where you left off.

If anything's confusing or not working the way you expect, just reply to this email (or message us on {{support_channel}}) — we'll personally help you get set up, no charge.

Looking forward to seeing your page live.

{{your_name}}
PageZaper

**P.S.** — if you started and got stuck on something specific, tell us what it was. That helps us fix it for everyone, not just you.
